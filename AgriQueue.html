📸 AgriVerify — Verified Crop Assessment

Do not claim that AI can prove every image is genuine. Use layered risk reduction.

AQ-READY

2. Capture crop live inside the app
3. Include code near crop + capture multiple angles
4. Check duplicate/reused submissions and consistency signals
5. AI quality screening → confidence → verified or manual review

“A normal upload can be taken from Google, so we do not blindly trust image classification. We use a session-specific challenge, live capture workflow, multiple angles and duplicate screening. Suspicious or low-confidence cases are marked for manual verification.”
